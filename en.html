Privacy Policy

App: Hanibal · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Hanibal mobile app (the "App"), published by Hanibal-AI ("we"), collects, why, and what your rights are. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Data controller

Hanibal-AI. Contact: contact@hanibal.fr

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and account identifier, provided at sign-up.
  • Usage and diagnostics data: interactions with the App, crash reports, performance data, device model, OS version, language.
  • Advertising identifiers: the device Advertising ID and related data, used to serve ads.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or health or financial data.

3. Purposes

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • Provide, maintain and improve the App.
  • Measure usage, detect and fix bugs.
  • Display advertising.
  • Contact you for support or important service-related information.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Performance of a contract (account and service), legitimate interests (security, diagnostics, improvement), consent (personalized ads and advertising identifiers, which you can withdraw at any time) and legal obligation where applicable.

5. Third-party services

The App uses third-party services that may collect data under their own policies, including:

  • Google Play Services – privacy policy
  • Analytics and crash reporting (e.g. Google Firebase) – information
  • Advertising services (e.g. Google AdMob) – information

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Sharing

Your data is shared only with the providers above, acting on our behalf, or where required by law.

7. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted within a reasonable time after account deletion, unless the law requires otherwise. Analytics data is retained according to each provider's schedule.

8. Your rights and account deletion

You may at any time request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, object to its processing, or withdraw your consent. To request deletion of your account and data, email contact@hanibal.fr from the address linked to your account; we reply within 30 days.

You can turn off ads personalization and reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads). You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Security

We apply reasonable measures (encrypted transfers over HTTPS, restricted access) to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 15 in France without parental consent). We do not knowingly collect their data; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Union, with appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

12. Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change and, for significant changes, you will be notified.
